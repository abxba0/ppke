## Context

The **Personal Philosophical Knowledge Engine (PPKE)** is an advanced CLI 
tool designed for deep logical parsing and restructuring of philosophical 
texts. The current design is tightly coupled to philosophy-specific logic, 
which prevents broader adoption as a universal framework. This metaprompt 
outlines the plan to generalize PPKE into a domain-agnostic knowledge engine
 with plugin architecture.



---



## Goals

1. **Generalization**: Refactor PPKE for domain-agnostic functionality, 
enabling use in domains like law, medicine, and science.

2. **Plugin Architecture**: Implement a 2-tier plugin system:

   - **Tier 1 (Official)**: Verified and curated by maintainers.

   - **Tier 2 (Custom)**: User-submitted extensions that can be promoted 
after review.

3. **Standardized Schema**: Replace hardcoded structures with Pydantic-based
 dynamic schemas.

4. **Upgrade Licensing**: Apply **Apache 2.0 License** to ensure recognition
 while maintaining commercial usage compatibility.

5. **Obsidian Integration**: Maintain seamless integration for Obsidian 
Vault as a backend.



---



## Ralph Wiggum Methodology

### **Iterative Process:**

For every phase:

1. **Execute** the planned task.

2. **Audit** for logical, functional, and integration errors.

3. **Iterate** until the task complies with the specification.

4. **Validate** final outputs rigorously in real-world scenarios.
